"Evening Songbird" Concert Visual Identity

UI / UX, packaging & product design

Back

I was commissioned to design promotional graphics for a musical concert in Shanghai, China. Drawing inspiration from the performers' Japanese-influenced style, I created a night-themed design featuring pixelated hanabi (firework) elements. The visuals balance flat and dimensional objects, complemented by stylized depictions of the two musicians, blending traditional motifs with a modern twist.
