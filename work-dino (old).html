Dino: Branding & Website Design

UI / UX, Branding

Back

Dino.live is a Web3 event platform that uses token-gating and E-Token rewards to create branded, high-engagement experiences for hosts and attendees.

I led the end-to-end UI design for both the landing page and web app, collaborating closely with product and engineering to deliver Dino’s MVP in August 2023. This included developing a scalable design system and iterating on high-fidelity prototypes for real-time deployment at Dino’s own sponsored events. As the sole designer, I also defined Dino’s brand identity—establishing a playful, techno-inspired aesthetic across both product and marketing materials.
