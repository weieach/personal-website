Wu Chien-Shiung — Biography poster

Poster Design

Back

This poster commemorates experimental physicist Wu Chien-Shiung, who is recognized as one of the most influential yet under-acknowledged figures in 20th-century physics: Although her research was foundational to discoveries credited to her male peers, her contributions were long marginalized. By spotlighting a quote from a 1964 speech she delivered at MIT, the design treats her words as both visual and conceptual anchors, aiming to restore visibility to her lasting, interdisciplinary impact on science.
