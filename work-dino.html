Dino: Web UI Design

Dino is an event discovery and coordination platform built for the Web3 ecosystem, replacing makeshift event tracking with a unified, community-driven web app.

  • Role

    Visual & product designer

  • Timeline

    January - December 2023

  • Tools

    Figma

I owned the branding, design system, and responsive UI across Dino’s landing page, web app, and conference campaigns. Throughout the build, I partnered closely with full-stack engineers and product managers to take each iteration from prototype to production.

Back

Core Problems

  • Low ecosystem visibility

    Organizers and participants had no centralized platform to aggregate major event series, causing attendees to miss relevant sessions and side events.

  • Reliance on static spreadsheets

    Event coordination ran on messy, shared spreadsheets that were neither comprehensive nor mobile-friendly.

  • No post-event continuity

    Hosts had no seamless way to integrate NFTs for identity verification and community building, limiting long-term engagement between participants.

Research question

How might we centralize Web3 event discovery while building lasting ties between hosts and attendees?

Results

Through tight coordination between design, product, and engineering, we shipped the MVP on schedule in August 2023—six months after I joined to lead the design process. Before the company pivoted in late 2023, the platform generated 5,000+ visits, 1,000+ user sign-ups, and helped secure $100K in grants from MiraclePlus (formerly YC China).

I also extended Dino’s brand identity to major industry events, creating illustrations, posters, and stage backdrops for Stanford Blockchain Conference 2023 (“Ignite Stanford”) and NFT NYC 2023 (“Skytop”). These campaigns drove ~800 guest registrations on Dino and boosted social engagement by 3,000+ Twitter followers.
